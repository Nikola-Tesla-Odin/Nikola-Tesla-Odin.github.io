About me

Hindi has been my life's subject

I studied Hindi literature from my B.A. through my M.A. at Banasthali Vidyapith, one of India's best-known women's universities, and finished the M.A. ranked first in my program with a Gold Medal (93.8%). Today I am a Ph.D. Research Scholar in Hindi Literature there.

I am also a trained teacher. My B.Ed. (90%) included classroom teaching internships in a private school and a government senior secondary school, where I taught grammar, prose and poetry, planned lessons and assessed students' written and spoken Hindi.

What I love most is the moment a student first reads a sentence on their own, or laughs at a Hindi joke with their grandparents. I bring that warmth, along with real depth in the language, to every lesson.

Questions parents ask

FAQ

My child understands Hindi but won't speak it. Can you help?

Yes, this is the most common situation. We start with fun, low-pressure conversation about things your child already loves, so speaking feels natural rather than like a test.

My child can't read Devanagari at all. Is that okay?

Completely. We begin with letters and sounds, using games and the shared whiteboard, and build up to reading short stories.

Do you teach for the VCE, HSC or WA ATAR Hindi exams?

Yes. Lessons follow your syllabus, with grammar, writing practice with detailed feedback, oral exam rehearsal and in-depth help with literature.

How long are lessons, and how often?

Usually 45–60 minutes for older students and 30–45 minutes for younger children, once or twice a week. We decide together after the trial.

What do we need for online lessons?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, headphones if possible and a stable internet connection. I'll send the Zoom or Google Meet link before each lesson.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on lesson length and frequency. Message me on WhatsApp after the free trial and I'll share the options.
