How it works

From token to talking in four steps

01

Your token

Only a token's creator can give it a voice, so nobody can speak for yours.

02

A personality

Choose a ready voice or describe a new one. Claude writes its style, themes and limits.

03

Your channels

Connect your X account (through Buffer) and a Telegram channel.

04

It posts

Every 2 to 24 hours, an original line and a matching quote card, around the clock.

It runs in your own free GitHub account with your own keys. Nobody else ever holds them.

Rules every bot keeps

A voice, never a shill

No price talk

No charts, gains or predictions, and never "buy", "sell" or "hold". Nothing here is financial advice.

No tagging strangers

Token bots never @-mention other accounts, so they don't read as spam.

Original lines only

Every post is new writing in the voice, never a real quotation passed off as genuine.

A hard spend cap

Each bot stops itself at its monthly budget. $1.50 a month covers a post every 2 hours.
