YouTube, just the music.

You open YouTube for one song and end up 40 minutes deep in Shorts. Music Focus turns YouTube into a music player: no video, no feed, no Shorts, no comments.

Everything that isn't music, gone

Album art, not video

A clean now-playing card with real music controls: progress, previous / next, repeat and volume.

Classic mode

A retro click-wheel player. Spin it for volume, press the centre to seek. It even clicks. MENU → Brick for a game while the song plays.

No rabbit holes

The home feed becomes a search box. Recommendations, comments, end screens and Shorts disappear.

Your week, on a receipt

Every week Music Focus prints a Focus Receipt: hours listened, top tracks, top artist, rabbit holes dodged and Shorts watched. Copy it straight into a post.

Stats stay on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Privacy policy

Install it now

While the Chrome Web Store listing is in review, install it by hand in about a minute:

  1. Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Refresh YouTube. Pin Music Focus from the puzzle-piece menu to switch modes.
