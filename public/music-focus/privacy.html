Music Focus privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Music Focus is a browser extension by Nikola Tesla Studio that changes how youtube.com looks so it works like a music player. In short: it collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere, except a license key you choose to activate if you buy Music Focus Pro.

What the extension stores

To show your weekly Focus Receipt, Music Focus keeps these in your browser's extension storage, on your device only:

  • your settings (whether Music mode is on, and which player style you picked);
  • for each week: total listening time, and for each song you played, its YouTube video ID, title, artist and how long you listened;
  • counts of recommendations and Shorts it hid, and of Shorts you opened while Music mode was off.

What it doesn't do

  • It has no servers. None of the data above is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • It doesn't use analytics, tracking or ads, and doesn't sell or share data.
  • It doesn't read your YouTube account, history, passwords or anything on other websites. It only runs on www.youtube.com.

Music Focus Pro (optional purchase)

Music Focus Pro is a one-time purchase sold by Lemon Squeezy, which handles the payment as merchant of record under its own privacy policy. Music Focus never sees your payment details.

When you activate a Pro license key, the extension sends that key and a device label (for example "Music Focus on Chrome (Mac)") to Lemon Squeezy to confirm it, and re-checks it about once a week. Nothing else is sent. A 10-minute free trial runs entirely on your device.

Sharing your receipt

A Focus Receipt image leaves your device only if you choose to copy, download or post it. Posting opens X with suggested text that you can edit before posting.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
